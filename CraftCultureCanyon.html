Overview

Craft, Culture & Canyon Tour — Artistry, heritage, and nature woven into one enriching day. Discover Malindi’s cultural roots, interact with local artisans, and explore the breathtaking Marafa Canyon.

  • Cultural orientation and guided town exploration
  • Visit Muungano Handcraft Market and meet artisans
  • Explore Swahili heritage and architecture
  • Lunch at a reputable local restaurant
  • Guided storytelling experience at Marafa Canyon
  • Comfortable return transfers included
